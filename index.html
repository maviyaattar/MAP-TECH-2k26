About MAP-TECH 2K26

MAP-TECH 2K26 is a prestigious state-level technical event organized by Maulana Azad Polytechnic, Solapur. This event brings together bright minds from across the state to showcase their technical prowess, innovative ideas, and problem-solving capabilities.

Our event features three exciting categories designed to challenge and inspire participants:

  • Paper Presentation: Share your research and innovative solutions to real-world technical problems
  • Poster Presentation: Visually communicate your technical concepts and innovations
  • Technical Quiz: Test your technical knowledge and compete with the best minds

Join us in this celebration of technical excellence and innovation. Network with peers, learn from experts, and showcase your skills on a state-level platform.
