User Guide & Instructions

Step 1: Fill Registration Form

Complete all required fields in the registration form:

  • Institute Name: Enter your institution's full name (no numbers allowed)
  • Department: Select your department from the dropdown
  • Full Name: Enter your complete name with first and last name (no numbers allowed, minimum 8 characters)
  • WhatsApp Number: Provide a valid 10-digit WhatsApp number starting with 6-9
  • Email: Enter a valid email address
  • Event/Workshop: Choose the event you want to participate in

Step 2: Make Payment

Complete the registration fee payment of ₹60 using either method:

  • Scan the QR code with any UPI app
  • Click on PhonePe or Paytm button to pay directly through the app

Step 3: Upload Payment Proof

After successful payment:

  • Take a screenshot of your payment confirmation
  • Click "Click to Upload Payment Proof" button
  • Select the screenshot from your device
  • Wait for the preview to appear confirming your upload

Step 4: Submit & Confirm

Once all fields are filled and payment proof is uploaded:

  • Click "Submit Registration" button
  • Wait for the upload process to complete
  • You'll be redirected to a thank you page
  • Save your confirmation ID for future reference

PhonePe Payment

To pay using PhonePe:

  • Click the "Pay with PhonePe" button
  • Your PhonePe app will open automatically
  • Verify the payment details (Amount: ₹60, Recipient: MAPTECH2K26)
  • Complete the payment using your preferred method
  • Take a screenshot of the payment confirmation

Paytm Payment

To pay using Paytm:

  • Click the "Pay with Paytm" button
  • Your Paytm app will open automatically
  • Verify the payment details (Amount: ₹60, Recipient: MAPTECH2K26)
  • Complete the payment using your Paytm wallet or UPI
  • Take a screenshot of the payment confirmation

QR Code Payment

To pay by scanning QR code:

  • Open any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)
  • Select "Scan QR Code" option
  • Scan the QR code displayed on the page
  • Verify amount is ₹60
  • Complete the payment
  • Take a screenshot of the payment confirmation

Payment Tips

  • Ensure you have sufficient balance before initiating payment
  • Double-check the recipient details before confirming payment
  • Keep your payment screenshot safe - you'll need it for upload
  • Payment verification may take 24-48 hours
  • Do not share your payment screenshot publicly

What if I don't have PhonePe or Paytm?

You can use any UPI app to scan the QR code displayed on the page. All UPI apps (Google Pay, BHIM, etc.) will work with the QR code.

What payment proof should I upload?

Upload a clear screenshot of your payment confirmation that shows:

  • Transaction ID or reference number
  • Payment amount (₹60)
  • Recipient name (MAPTECH2K26)
  • Date and time of transaction

Can I edit my registration after submission?

No, registration details cannot be edited after submission. Please ensure all information is correct before submitting. For any corrections, contact us via WhatsApp.

How long does payment verification take?

Payment verification is done manually by our admin team and typically takes 24-48 hours. You'll receive a WhatsApp confirmation once verified.

When will I get a response to my WhatsApp query?

Our teachers review and respond to WhatsApp queries within 24-48 hours. Please be patient and avoid sending multiple messages for the same query.

What if my payment is deducted but registration fails?

In rare cases of payment success but registration failure, your payment proof will be saved. Contact us immediately via WhatsApp with your transaction details.
